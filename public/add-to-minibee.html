Add to Minibee

1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Add to Minibee

2a. Many items at once (fastest):

  1. Search on mobile.yangkeduo.com (Pinduoduo), e.g. "ins风可爱发夹" or "韩版小众包包". Scroll down to load more items.
  2. Click the Add to Minibee bookmark. All items from the search show in a grid.
  3. Tap the items you like, then Save selected. Photos, English name, price and sizes are filled in automatically.

2b. One item (to pick photos and name yourself):

  1. Open the item's page on Pinduoduo and click Add to Minibee.
  2. Tap the photos to use (one item, no text photos), check the name, click Save.

New items show on the site after a page refresh. To fix a name, price or photo, or to delete an item, change its row in the "Minibee Products" Google Sheet.
